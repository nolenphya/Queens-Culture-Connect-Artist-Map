The Queens Arts and Culture Map

Welcome to the Queens Arts and Culture Map

In 2024, Flushing Town Hall was invited by the New York State Council on the Arts (NYSCA) to lead The Queens Cultural Mapping Initiative, designed to document the rich and diverse artistic landscape of Queens.

How to use the map

  • 🔍 Explore Queens: Zoom into neighborhoods to see cultural pins and toggle boundaries via the legend checkboxes.
  • 🚇 Transit Network: Zoom past level 14 to see subway station labels. Click any station dot to view incoming routes.
